# FAQ - Frequently Asked Questions

## General Questions

### What is AI Treasure Hunt?
AI Treasure Hunt is a gamified exploration app that uses AI to generate personalized treasure hunts based on your location, interests, and preferences. Users explore nearby places, collect treasures, earn XP, and climb the leaderboard.

### Who can use the app?
Anyone 13 years or older can use AI Treasure Hunt. The app is designed for explorers of all ages who love discovering new places.

### Is the app free?
Yes! AI Treasure Hunt is free to download and play. We plan to offer optional premium features in future versions.

### How often do I get new treasures?
You get one new treasure every 24 hours. You can also explore previously generated treasures or nearby treasures on the map.

### Is the app available offline?
Currently, the app requires internet for most features. Offline mode is planned for a future release.

## Gameplay

### How do I collect treasures?
1. Check the daily treasure card on home screen
2. View location on map
3. Navigate to the location
4. Arrive and tap "Collect Treasure"
5. Earn XP and rewards

### What is XP?
XP (Experience Points) is earned by discovering treasures. Accumulate XP to level up and unlock achievements.

### What are levels?
Levels progress from 1-50. Each level requires 500 XP. Higher levels unlock special achievements and badges.

### What are achievements and badges?
Achievements are milestones you unlock (e.g., "First Steps" for discovering your first treasure). Badges are visual rewards displayed on your profile.

### What is a daily streak?
A streak counts consecutive days you discover treasures. Streaks give bonus XP multipliers (2x for 7+ day streaks).

## Features

### How does the AI generate treasures?
Our AI uses Gemini API to analyze your location, interests, weather, and nearby places to create unique, personalized treasures.

### Can I share treasures?
Yes! We're adding social sharing features. You'll soon be able to share discoveries with friends.

### Can I filter treasures by category?
Yes! Treasures are categorized (Cafe, Park, Temple, etc.). Filter on the map screen.

### How does distance calculation work?
We use your GPS location and calculate straight-line distance. Walking distance may vary.

## Technical Issues

### The app crashes on startup
- Ensure you're using the latest version
- Clear app cache in Settings
- Reinstall the app
- Check device has sufficient storage

### Maps aren't loading
- Check internet connection
- Verify Google Maps API key is correct
- Restart the app
- Update Google Play Services (Android)

### Location not working
- Enable location permissions in Settings
- Ensure GPS is enabled
- Try clearing app cache
- Restart your device

### Notifications not working
- Enable notifications in app settings
- Check system notification settings
- Ensure internet connection
- Reinstall app if needed

### Slow performance
- Close other apps
- Clear app cache
- Restart your device
- Check available storage
- Ensure good internet connection

## Troubleshooting

### "No internet connection" error
- Check WiFi or mobile data connection
- Disable VPN if using one
- Try switching networks

### "Firebase error"
- Ensure Firebase is properly configured
- Check internet connection
- Try force closing and reopening app

### "Permission denied"
- Grant required permissions in Settings
- Go to App Permissions
- Enable Camera, Location, and Storage as needed

### "Can't login"
- Check email/password
- Try "Forgot Password"
- Try Google Sign-In
- Contact support if issue persists
