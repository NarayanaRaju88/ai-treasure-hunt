# Community Guidelines

## Our Mission

Create a welcoming, inclusive community of treasure hunters who explore their world, discover amazing places, and share their adventures.

## Be Respectful

✅ **DO**:
- Treat others with kindness and respect
- Welcome diverse perspectives
- Give constructive feedback
- Help new members
- Celebrate others' discoveries

❌ **DON'T**:
- Use harsh or offensive language
- Discriminate based on any characteristic
- Harass or bully other members
- Make assumptions about others
- Gatekeep or exclude others

## Keep It Safe

✅ **DO**:
- Explore in safe, public areas
- Respect private property
- Follow local laws and regulations
- Be aware of your surroundings
- Stay safe while walking

❌ **DON'T**:
- Trespass on private property
- Enter dangerous areas
- Share personal information
- Meet strangers alone
- Share exact home address

## Share Responsibly

✅ **DO**:
- Get permission before sharing others' photos
- Credit original locations
- Share interesting discoveries
- Encourage others to explore
- Respect local culture

❌ **DON'T**:
- Share others' personal information
- Post inappropriate content
- Spam or advertise
- Share copyrighted material
- Exploit locations for profit

## Respect Privacy

✅ **DO**:
- Respect others' privacy in photos
- Ask before taking pictures
- Blur faces when sharing
- Keep personal info private
- Delete unwanted data

❌ **DON'T**:
- Post photos without consent
- Share phone numbers or addresses
- Track or stalk other users
- Take non-consensual photos
- Share others' discoveries as your own

## Code of Conduct

### Zero Tolerance for:
- Harassment of any kind
- Hate speech or discrimination
- Sexual harassment or exploitation
- Threats or violence
- Illegal activity

### Violations Result in:
- First offense: Warning
- Second offense: Temporary ban (7 days)
- Third offense: Permanent ban

## Reporting Issues

### How to Report

1. **In-app**: Use the "Report" button
2. **Email**: report@example.com
3. **Twitter**: @AITreasureHunt
4. **GitHub**: Create an issue

### What to Include
- Clear description of issue
- Username(s) involved
- Screenshots if applicable
- Timestamp and location
- Your contact information

### Response Time
- Urgent issues: 24 hours
- Standard issues: 48-72 hours
- Community issues: 1-2 weeks

## Moderation

### Our Moderators
- Enforce community guidelines
- Review reports fairly
- Maintain safe environment
- Support community growth

### Moderation Actions
- Warning
- Comment removal
- Account suspension (temporary)
- Account ban (permanent)

## Accessibility

✅ **DO**:
- Include image descriptions
- Use clear language
- Provide captions for videos
- Consider color contrast
- Support multiple formats

## Environmental Responsibility

✅ **DO**:
- Leave no trace
- Respect nature
- Clean up after yourself
- Protect wildlife
- Conserve resources

❌ **DON'T**:
- Litter or pollute
- Disturb animals
- Pick protected plants
- Damage historical sites
- Ignore "No Entry" signs

## Diversity & Inclusion

We celebrate:
- All ages and abilities
- All backgrounds and cultures
- All genders and identities
- All perspectives and experiences
- All levels of exploration

We're committed to:
- Inclusive design
- Diverse representation
- Accessible features
- Welcoming environment
- Equal opportunities
